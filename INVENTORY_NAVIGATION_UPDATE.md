# Inventory navigation update

The previous combined Inventory & Product Batches page has been split into two focused admin views under one Inventory sidebar parent.

- `/admin/inventory` — Inventory view: stock metrics, search/health filters, stock movement history, inventory detail, adjustments and stock transfers.
- `/admin/inventory/product-batches` — Product batches: receiving history, retail/wholesale/cost prices, remaining quantities and the Add Product Batch workflow.

The Inventory sidebar entry now expands to show both child pages, so batch history no longer requires scrolling below the full stock ledger.
