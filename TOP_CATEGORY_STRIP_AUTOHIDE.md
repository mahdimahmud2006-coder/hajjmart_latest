# Top category strip auto-hide refinement

- The desktop department/category strip is visible only while the visitor is at the top of the storefront.
- After scrolling roughly 96px into the page, the strip collapses, fades, and slides upward smoothly.
- The main HajjMart header remains usable while scrolling.
- Returning to the top restores the category strip automatically.
- Any open mega-menu is force-hidden while the strip is collapsed, preventing overlap artifacts.
- Reduced-motion users receive an effectively instant transition.
